\section*{Reproducibility Statement}

The release (scenes, scorers, scripts, per-episode data, pre-registrations, \texttt{supp/cells.csv} and a supplementary video with rendered episodes for T1, T2, T3, T6a and T6b and the humanoid case study) recomputes the tabletop rates of Table~\ref{tab:main}; a golden test re-scores every released cell exactly.
Policies run unmodified in IsaacLab-Arena~0.3 on Isaac Sim~6.0 and Isaac Lab~3.0 \citep{ref15,ref14}: \nolinkurl{pi05_droid_jointpos} (openpi, served under the configuration \nolinkurl{pi05_droid_jointpos_polaris}), \nolinkurl{pi0_droid_jointpos_polaris} and \nolinkurl{pi0_fast_droid_jointpos_polaris} \citep{ref66}, \texttt{nvidia/GR00T-N1.6-DROID} and GN1x-Tuned-Arena-G1-Loco-Manipulation \citep{ref13}.
Tabletop seeds are 42 and 7; 86 of the 567 pooled cells use further seeds, listed per cell in \texttt{supp/cells.csv} (G1: 42, 7 and 123).
A seed labels a cell and does not fix its episodes, so a rerun gives different episodes.
Tabletop episodes end when the environment's success condition holds or after 35\,s (90\,s for most \grd{} T1--T4 cells; 20\,s for four \pizero{} serving cells).

\section*{Ethics Statement}

All experiments are in simulation with proxy humans; no human subjects, personal data or physical robots were involved.
The benchmark exists to expose safety failures of released VLA policies so that they can be measured and fixed.
The standards we cite are measurement references, not certification claims, and no result should be read as a safety rating of a product.
We see limited dual-use risk: the hazards are ordinary household objects, and the failure modes are observable by anyone who runs these policies.
