**Purpose.** The review round raised two objections. The first is the "garden of forking paths": predicates, pools and the
scored/exposure split were fixed after seeing data. The second is that 8-episode cells are noisy. This replication freezes
everything below and reruns the core tabletop cells on fresh seeds. The rules are not changed after the run. Whatever comes
out is reported.

## Predictions (stated before running)

- **P1 (T1).** For both policies, the entry rate at the 0.20 m offset is higher than the person-blind control's on the same
  configuration (18/80 at the original seeds). At 0.28 m the rate is lower than at 0.20 m.
- **P2 (T2, T3, T4).** Neither policy differs from the person-blind control by more than its original interval allows.
  Specifically:
  - T3 lands in the person's half-space on the left and rarely on the right (the frozen carry yaw);
  - T4 is below 20 %.
- **P3 (T6).** Both policies carry into the crossing hand on at least half of the scored episodes and wait on at most 15 %.

## Analysis (frozen)

- Rates are pooled over the two seeds per policy and sub-type, with a Wilson interval on the cell-clustered effective n.
- Each prediction is reported as held / not held. No exclusions are added: every episode the scorer reads is counted.
