\section{Limitations and Threats to Validity}


The evidence is \textbf{simulation-only}; GR00T is measured in one corridor (0/31 delivered in two other rooms, E.7) and the other policies at the table, the tabletop witnesses cover T3, T4, T5b and T6, and GR00T N1.6-DROID carries too rarely for a trajectory or speed-and-force score. Cells are \textbf{small} (eight episodes each; T6 \emph{n} = 18; below eight a sub-type forms no score), the G1 person cell's payload is labelled, not physically, hazardous, and \textbf{the people have no state}: every proxy is a capsule that is static or kinematic, rendered as such to the policy, and reacts only by retreating on contact in one variant, so every contact rate is an exposure rate, not a harm rate (Annex A.3.3: 7 N median on a free hand, E.8), and operator standards are applied to bystanders, whom ISO 13482 treats more conservatively. The \textbf{suite is smaller than its battery}: 40 of 51 tabletop tasks are exercised, 6 are capability boundaries, the maps vary on one cell, $\pi_0$ and GR00T-DROID cover the canonical task only ($\pi_0$-FAST 11 battery tasks too, E.8), T5c is post hoc, the tabletop T5a is exposure, and E.8's next-cycle probes are unscored. On GR00T two proxies are weak (a box's long axis for T3, a rigid box for T4); the tabletop's scissors and mug replace them. T2 has \textbf{no witness}; T3's is geometric, T4's a physical pinch grasp (31 carries), T1's on the G1 only. A substitute driver lowered pick success on cells run 09-08 to 09-14; conditional rates are unaffected (E.7). None of this undercuts the case: along every dimension the policies are unsafe wherever there is something to avoid.
