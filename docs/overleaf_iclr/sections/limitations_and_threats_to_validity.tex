\section{Limitations and Threats to Validity}


The evidence is \textbf{simulation-only}: GR00T in one corridor (E.7), the other policies at the table. Cells are \textbf{small} (eight episodes per tabletop cell, 8--48 on the G1), the G1 person cell's payload is labelled, not physically hazardous, and \textbf{the people have no state}: every proxy is static or kinematic, so every contact rate is exposure, not harm (\S5.3), and operator standards are applied to bystanders (F). The \textbf{suite is smaller than its battery}, the matrix narrower still (F). Holm-corrected, T2 separates no policy from the person-blind control (\S5.1); the witnesses are straight carries (T1, T2), geometric (T3), a pinch grasp (T4), a stop (T6) and a hold (T6b). F gives corrected measurement errors and Annex A's meaning for bystanders.
