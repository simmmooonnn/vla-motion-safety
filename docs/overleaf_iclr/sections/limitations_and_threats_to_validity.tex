\section{Limitations and Threats to Validity}


We state the boundaries plainly (Appendix F expands each). The evidence is \textbf{simulation-only}; GR00T is measured in the corridor and the other policies at the table, the tabletop witnesses cover T3, T4, T5b and T6, and GR00T N1.6-DROID carries too rarely for a trajectory or speed-and-force score. Cells are \textbf{small} (eight episodes each; T5a \emph{n} = 6, T6 \emph{n} = 11; below eight a sub-type prints as a count and forms no score), the G1 person cell's payload is labelled, not physically, hazardous, and \textbf{the people have no state}: every proxy is a capsule that is static or kinematic, rendered as such to the policy, and reacts only by retreating on contact in one variant, so every contact rate is an exposure rate, not a harm rate, and the operator standards scored against are applied to untrained bystanders, whom ISO 13482 would treat more conservatively. The \textbf{suite is smaller than its battery}: 36 of 47 tabletop tasks are exercised, 6 are capability boundaries, the maps vary on one cell, $\pi_0$ and GR00T-DROID cover the canonical task only, T5c is post hoc, the tabletop T5a is exposure, and the next-cycle probes of Appendix E.8 are unscored. On GR00T two proxies are weak --- a box's long axis for a hazardous axis (T3), a rigid box that cannot spill (T4) --- which the tabletop's scissors and mug replace. T2 has \textbf{no witness}; T3 has a geometric scripted witness on the tabletop, T4 a physical pinch-grasp witness based on 31 carried episodes, and T1 a witness on the G1 only. The on-path ablations are \textbf{ceiling-limited}; the non-ceiling ablation detects only large effects. Cells run 2026-09-08 to 09-14 used a substitute driver that lowered pick success; their conditional rates are unaffected (Appendix E.7). Keep-out radii are \textbf{illustrative} (Appendix D). None of this undercuts the case: along every dimension the policies are unsafe wherever there is something to avoid.
