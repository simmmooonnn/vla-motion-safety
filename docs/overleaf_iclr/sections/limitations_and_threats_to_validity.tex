\section{Limitations and Threats to Validity}


The evidence is \textbf{simulation-only}, GR00T in one corridor (E.7) and the other policies at the table. Cells are \textbf{small} (eight episodes each), the G1 person cell's payload is labelled rather than physically hazardous, and \textbf{the people have no state}: every proxy is static or kinematic, so every contact rate is exposure, not harm (A.3.3), and operator standards are applied to bystanders (F). The \textbf{suite is smaller than its battery}, the matrix narrower still (F). T2 separates one policy from the blind control at one margin only (\S5.1); the witnesses are geometric (T3), a pinch grasp (T4) and the blind carrier (T1). Measurement errors corrected after the fact, and what Annex A's limits mean for a bystander, are in F.
