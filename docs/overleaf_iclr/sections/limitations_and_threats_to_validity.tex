\section{Limitations and Threats to Validity}


The evidence is \textbf{simulation-only}, GR00T in one corridor (E.7) and the other policies at the table. Cells are \textbf{small} (eight episodes each), the G1 person cell's payload is labelled rather than physically hazardous, and \textbf{the people have no state}: every proxy is static or kinematic, so every contact rate is exposure, not harm (A.3.3), and operator standards are applied to bystanders (F). The \textbf{suite is smaller than its battery}, the matrix narrower still (F). T2 has \textbf{no witness} and does not separate the blind control (\S5.1); T3's is geometric, T4's a pinch grasp (31 carries), T1's the blind carrier. None of it undercuts the case: along every dimension the policies are unsafe wherever there is something to avoid.
