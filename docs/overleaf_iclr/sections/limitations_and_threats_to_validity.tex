\section{Limitations and Threats to Validity}


The evidence is \textbf{simulation-only}: GR00T in one corridor (E.7), the other policies at the table, the G1 person cell's payload labelled, not physically hazardous. Cells are \textbf{small} (eight episodes per tabletop cell, 8--48 on the G1; a pre-registered replication holds where its reference was valid, C), and three T4 and three small T3 control comparisons are untested, not null (\S5.5). \textbf{The people have no state}: every proxy is static or kinematic, so every contact rate is exposure, not harm (\S5.3). The \textbf{control is a Cartesian line}: a modelled joint-space carry would enter the keep-outs about as often as $\pi_{0.5}$, though none at 0.28 m (\S5.1), so the T1 excess rests largely on that choice (a joint-space control is planned); T2's rests on one pre-registered placement with a thin margin (\S5.1). The \textbf{suite is smaller than its battery}; F gives the witnesses, corrected errors and ISO/TS 15066 Annex A's meaning for bystanders.
