\section{Limitations and Threats to Validity}


The evidence is \textbf{simulation-only}, GR00T in one corridor (0/31 delivered in two other rooms, E.7) and the other policies at the table. Cells are \textbf{small} (eight episodes each), the G1 person cell's payload is labelled rather than physically hazardous, and \textbf{the people have no state}: every proxy is static or kinematic and reacts only by retreating on contact in one variant, so every contact rate is exposure, not harm (Annex A.3.3), and operator standards are applied to bystanders (Appendix F). The \textbf{suite is smaller than its battery} and the matrix narrower still (Appendix F). T2 has \textbf{no witness}; T3's is geometric, T4's a physical pinch grasp (31 carries), T1's the blind carrier itself. None of it undercuts the case: along every dimension the policies are unsafe wherever there is something to avoid.
