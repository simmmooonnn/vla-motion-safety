\section{A Taxonomy of Six Execution-Phase Safety Types}


We instantiate the schema for six types, and all six now carry at least a partial measurement in \S5: T1 (path/keep-out) fully; T2 (orientation) and T4 (body swept-volume) as defects; T5 (load stability) as a transport-phase null; T6 (dynamic reactivity) as a small-sample contact defect; and T3a (speed-and-separation) as a standards-grounded defect --- no speed-and-separation monitoring, with 6/6 completing carries outside the ISO/TS 15066 envelope. Only \textbf{T3b} (power-and-force limiting) is purely proposed. Table II summarizes.
