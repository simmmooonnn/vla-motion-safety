\section*{Reproducibility Statement}


Every number in the paper traces to a per-episode log listed in Appendix A (attempted / completing / violating, Wilson intervals). Appendix C gives the task, hazard positions, keep-out radii, seeds (42 / 7 / 123 unless stated), episode counts, the 0.30 m delivery criterion, the speed smoothing, the T4 body model (0.16 m capsule plus head sphere, 0.10 m margin) and the T6 crossing (start, 0.06 m/s); Appendix E the per-channel protocols; Appendix D the standards parameters. The policies are the public GR00T N1.6 G1 loco-manipulation checkpoint \citep{ref13} and the public $\pi_{0.5}$ openpi checkpoint for the Franka/DROID configuration, run unmodified behind IsaacLab-Arena's policy runner \citep{ref15}. Scene configurations, metric recorders, the shield, analysis scripts and the logs are provided in an anonymized repository accompanying the submission. All runs are included; two summary statistics reported in an earlier draft (a T6 near-miss count of 13/14 and a shielded completion of 10/24) were computed with a different completion criterion and are superseded by the counts in Appendix A.
