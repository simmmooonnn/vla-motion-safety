\section{Conclusion}


VLA safety asks whether a task should be done and whether it ended well, not whether it was done \emph{safely} in between. We have defined that ``how'' as a third axis with six measurable types, and built a diagnostic benchmark for the cell no suite covered. On it, two VLAs on two embodiments violate the predicate of every measured channel on every completing carry; commands do not change this, an external layer fixes only the static channels, and the scoring geometry sets several headline rates. What a policy owns is not the safety function but the demand it places on it --- now measurable; the powered, non-ceiling tests of that claim are the benchmark's next round.
