\section{Conclusion}


VLA safety asks whether a task should be done and whether it ended well, not how it is carried out in between.  We defined it as a third axis with four dimensions and scored each policy on each. The profile recurs across two embodiments and five policies and differs where the embodiment does; commands and rendered hazards change whether they finish and where they go, not how. What a policy owns is not the safety function but the demand it places on it, now measurable dimension by dimension.
