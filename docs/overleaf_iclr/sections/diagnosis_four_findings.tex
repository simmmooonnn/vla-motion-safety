\section{Diagnosis: Four Findings}


The ablations ask whether a prompt or a percept can move the policies, where execution-phase safety departs from collision avoidance.

\textbf{(i) A safety command changes completion; the violation does not detectably fall.} Among completing carries a safety command does not detectably lower the violation (T1, a hazard on the path: 8/8 and 7/7; T3: 8/12 and 11/14); per attempt the rates move only with completion (T6 30 $\rightarrow$ 25 \%; Fig. \ref{fig:fixability}). With the stove 0.28 m off the path, neither naming nor rendering lowers the violation (Table~\ref{tab:XI}); each shifts completing carries by about 3 cm --- naming away from a hidden stove (\emph{p} = 0.017; 0.49 on two new seeds), rendering toward it (\emph{p} = 0.013, 0.005) --- among completers whose share the arms change (29--68 \%). ``Do not spill the coffee'' tilts the mug past 45$^{\circ}$ on 30/32 carries ($\pi_0$-FAST 14/27) and ``tilt the mug as little as possible'' on 18/32 (21/28), against 3/32 (0/32) neutral and ``keep the mug upright'' alone on 5/32 (1/31); keep-the-\emph{bowl}-upright-so-it-does-not-spill does it as often as its \emph{mug} version (26/32, 27/32): the words act on the carry, ungrounded in their object (E.8).

\textbf{(ii) A visible hazard does not repel the path; the arm's path drifts whether or not one is there.} The humanoid's 2--5 cm shift toward a rendered stove (two substitute-driver seeds, replicated on two new ones; E.2) comes with a higher violation rate (17/51 against 15/83, \emph{p} = 0.06; new seeds 32/36 against 22/33, \emph{p} = 0.04). A twin designed for it settles the tabletop: a keep-out 0.20 m beside the transport is entered on 24/24 carries with its marker rendered and 22/24 without it on the far side, 6/24 and 1/24 on the near side ($\pi_0$-FAST 20/20, 21/23; 1/21, 0/21): the far-side bend is there whether or not anything is, and a rendered hazard does not repel it (E.8).

\textbf{(iii) Neither orientation nor speed is detectably conditioned on the person.} The carry yaw shows no systematic shift with bystander azimuth for GR00T, table side for $\pi_{0.5}$ (T3) or any rendering of the person on either embodiment (E.7, E.8); the carry speed shows no detectable change with the person present (T5a). On the humanoid the base command shows no detectable difference with a bystander present (12 episode pairs, \emph{p} = 0.28; E.1), a test with little power against a local detour.

\textbf{(iv) A moving person is walked into, and one who stops is struck, not avoided.} No deceleration precedes contact at 0.3--1.2 m/s (T6) or on 17/18 scored encounters (T6b), and a person who stops at first touch is struck on every carried encounter (\S5.4). That the competences are \emph{absent from the imitation training distribution} (\S2) is consistent with, not demonstrated by, our ablations.
