\section{Diagnosis: Four Findings}


The ablations ask whether a prompt or a percept can move the policies.

\textbf{(i) A safety command changes completion; the violation does not detectably fall.} Among completing carries the rate does not fall (T1, a hazard on the path: 8/8 and 7/7; T3: 8/12 and 11/14); per attempt the rates move only with completion (T6 30 $\rightarrow$ 25 \%; Fig. \ref{fig:fixability}). With the stove 0.28 m off the path, neither naming nor rendering lowers the violation (Table~\ref{tab:XI}); each shifts completing carries by about 3 cm --- naming away from a hidden stove (\emph{p} = 0.017; 0.49 on two new seeds), rendering toward it (\emph{p} = 0.013, 0.005) --- among completers whose share the arms change (29--68 \%). ``Do not spill the coffee'' and ``tilt the mug as little as possible'' raise the tilt (Table~\ref{tab:XIII}; the second past 45$^{\circ}$ on 18/32, $\pi_0$-FAST 21/28), ``keep the mug upright'' alone not detectably; keep-the-\emph{bowl}-upright-so-it-does-not-spill does it as often as its \emph{mug} version (26/32, 27/32), so the spill clause, not the object named, drives it. It tilts $\pi_0$'s mug too (10/12 against 1/13, mostly in one cell), not detectably GR00T N1.6-DROID's (2/9 against 6/12; E.8).

\textbf{(ii) A visible hazard does not repel the path; the arm's path drifts whether or not one is there.} The humanoid's 2--5 cm shift toward a rendered stove (two substitute-driver seeds, replicated on two new ones; E.2) comes with a higher violation rate (17/51 against 15/83, \emph{p} = 0.06; new seeds 32/36 against 22/33, \emph{p} = 0.04). On the tabletop a keep-out 0.20 m beside the transport is entered on 24/24 carries with its marker rendered and 22/24 without it on the far side, 6/24 and 1/24 on the near side ($\pi_0$-FAST 20/20, 21/23; 1/21, 0/21; E.8).

\textbf{(iii) Neither orientation nor speed is detectably conditioned on the person.} The carry yaw shows no systematic shift with bystander azimuth for GR00T, table side for $\pi_{0.5}$ (T3) or any rendering of the person on either embodiment (E.7, E.8); neither the carry speed (T5a) nor the humanoid's base command (12 episode pairs, \emph{p} = 0.28; E.1; a test with little power against a local detour) changes detectably with a person present.

\textbf{(iv) A moving person is walked into, and one who stops is struck, not avoided.} No deceleration precedes contact at 0.3--1.2 m/s (T6) or on 17/18 scored encounters (T6b), and a person who stops at first touch is struck on every carried encounter (\S5.4). That the competences are \emph{absent from the imitation training distribution} (\S2) is consistent with, not demonstrated by, our ablations.
