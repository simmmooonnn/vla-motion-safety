\section{Diagnosis: Four Findings}


\textbf{(i) A safety command changes completion; the violation does not detectably fall.} Among completing carries the rate does not fall (T1, a hazard on the path: 8/8 and 7/7; T3: 8/12 and 11/14); per attempt the rates move only with completion (T6 30 $\rightarrow$ 25 \%; Fig. \ref{fig:fixability}). With the stove 0.28 m off the path, neither naming nor rendering lowers the violation (Table~\ref{tab:XI}); naming's 3 cm shift away from a hidden stove did not replicate (E.2). A spill clause (``do not spill the coffee'') and ``tilt the mug as little as possible'' raise the tilt (the clause's tilt mostly late in the carry, near the bowl); ``keep the mug upright'' alone does not detectably (against the spill phrase, cell permutation \emph{p} = 0.0004 pooled over $\pi_{0.5}$ and $\pi_0$-FAST; \S5.2, E.8). The clause tilts $\pi_0$'s mug too (10/12 against 1/13, mostly in one cell), not detectably GR00T N1.6-DROID's (2/9 against 6/12; E.8).

\textbf{(ii) A visible hazard does not repel the path; the arm's path bends whether or not one is there.} The humanoid's path shifts 2--5 cm toward a rendered stove (substitute-driver seeds, replicated on two new ones; E.2), with more violations (17/51 against 15/83, \emph{p} = 0.06; 32/36 against 22/33, \emph{p} = 0.04). On the tabletop the policies' carries bend to the far side whatever the keep-out's side or rendering (\S5.1): $\pi_{0.5}$ enters one 0.20 m beside the transport on 24/24 carries with its marker rendered and 22/24 without on the far side, 6/24 and 1/24 on the near side ($\pi_0$-FAST alike; E.8).

\textbf{(iii) Neither orientation nor speed is detectably conditioned on the person.} The carry yaw shows no systematic shift with bystander azimuth for GR00T, table side for $\pi_{0.5}$ (T3) or any rendering of the person on either embodiment (E.7, E.8); neither the carry speed (T5a) nor the humanoid's base command (\emph{p} = 0.28, little power against a local detour; E.1) changes detectably with a person present.

\textbf{(iv) A moving person is walked into, and one who stops is struck, not avoided.} No deceleration precedes contact (T6, 0.3--1.2 m/s; T6b, 17/18 encounters), and one who stops at first touch is struck on every carried encounter (\S5.4). That the competences are \emph{absent from the imitation training distribution} (\S2) is consistent with, not demonstrated by, our ablations.
