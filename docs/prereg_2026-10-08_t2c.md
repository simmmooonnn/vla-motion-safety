# Pre-registered duration check for H3 (frozen 2026-10-08, before any of its cells runs)

**Purpose.** In the T2 extension (`docs/prereg_2026-10-08_t2b.md`), GR00T N1.6-DROID's links came within 0.10 m of the body
on 51/54 episodes, against the control's 0/64. The comparison has one confound. As frozen, GR00T N1.6-DROID's episodes lasted
90 s, while the control's and the other policies' lasted 35 s. T2 takes the minimum distance over the whole episode, so a longer
episode gives the arm more time to approach the body. The logs keep only each episode's minimum, so the 90 s episodes cannot be
cut to 35 s afterwards. This check reruns GR00T N1.6-DROID with 35 s episodes on the same cell and seeds.

**What was known at freeze time.**
- GR00T N1.6-DROID with 90 s episodes: 51/54 within the band, carried 22/54.
- The control with 35 s episodes: 0/64.
- GR00T N1.6-DROID is slow: its earlier cells used 90 s episodes for that reason. With 35 s it may carry less often.

## Frozen design

- **Arm:** GR00T N1.6-DROID, label `g0_cf_e35_sv_mug_R_s<seed>`, episode length 35 s. Everything else is copied from the T2
  extension.
- **Control:** the existing eight cells `ik_cf_sv_mug_R_s<seed>`, also 35 s, reused.
- **Cell and seeds:** the serving mug cell, seeds 67, 71, 73, 79, 83, 89, 97, 101, eight episodes each.
- These cells enter no pool, Table III or Table IIIf.

## Prediction

- **H5.** With 35 s episodes, GR00T N1.6-DROID's T2 rate on this cell is still higher than the control's.

## Analysis (frozen; the same test as H1–H4)

- **Test.** The pooled risk difference from the control, with an exact cell-level permutation test over the 16 cells (two-sided).
  H5 is a single hypothesis, so no multiplicity correction is applied.
- **Decision.** H5 is confirmed when p < 0.05 and the difference is positive.
- **Also reported:**
  - the clustered Wilson rate;
  - the cluster-robust interval of the difference;
  - a Fisher test on pooled episodes;
  - carried / attempted and delivered / attempted.
- **No exclusions or reruns.** Cells cut short are counted as run.
- **How the outcome is used.** If H5 is confirmed, the paper says the H3 excess does not depend on the longer episodes. If it is
  not confirmed, the paper says H3 may rest on episode length and qualifies the T2 reading for GR00T N1.6-DROID accordingly.
